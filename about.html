Our story

From a family kitchen to a café

Aaroh started in 2021 when two friends from Kozhikode wanted young people to taste the Malabar food their grandmothers cooked, without the formality of a big sit-down meal. Today it is a café where recipes stay traditional and the setting feels like yours.

Mission

To make Malabar cuisine part of everyday café culture, with honest ingredients and fair prices.

Vision

To be the first place people think of when they want coastal Kerala flavours with good coffee and good company.

Why Aaroh

Regional recipes

Malabar dishes cooked the traditional way, not “Kerala-style” shortcuts.

Café comfort

Wi-Fi, soft seating and a coffee menu for long catch-ups.

Local sourcing

Fish, coconut and spices from nearby coastal suppliers.
